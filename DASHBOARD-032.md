# 0.32.0-alpha / greetings, casting card, scheduled appearance, calendar

Home adds a responsive clock/greeting row, beside the clock when wide and stacked when narrow. Device-local time chooses Good morning (05:00-11:59), Good afternoon (12:00-17:59) or Good evening, with short friendly messages. Greeting/date and scheduled appearance are checked on resume and minute boundaries while the dashboard is active.

Home and native Cast setup include a Cast from your phone card with same-network, receiver-enabling and explicit DLNA (not Chromecast) instructions. Its QR encodes exactly https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bubblecast and is labelled as an installation/listing link, not a pairing code. The card does not claim device discovery, successful casting, verified music support or compatibility with every sender. QR pixels stay black/white in either UI theme. No external link is opened automatically.

Settings adds an automatic-mode checkbox, manual Light/Dark controls and separate local-time switch-time pickers. Defaults are manual Light, light at 07:00 and dark at 19:00; automatic mode must be enabled. Overnight/wrapped schedules are supported and equal switching times are rejected. Settings commits are checked. UI theme changes restyle registered native panel shapes, known background/text colours and monochrome UI glyphs without rebuilding the active page or applying a global inversion filter. Image/video/WebView/drawing/ink content is excluded from colour filtering, including photo cards and QR images. Original media pixels, PDF/colouring pages and custom drawing canvases retain their original colours. This is dashboard/native workspace appearance, not forced dark rendering of third-party websites, custom game canvases or the separate colouring/cast player's existing canvas UI. Dialog chrome still follows the existing platform theme.

Calendar autosave updates the selected cell's entry marker/preview in place instead of deleting and recreating 42 cells. Same-month selection reuses cells; month/locale identity changes rebuild the grid. Day-cell number/preview padding and font sizes fit measured cell dimensions, previews hide on small cells, and the dashboard's generic minimum-font normalization no longer enlarges fitted cell contents after layout. Weekday header and minimum row space account for font scale. Existing entry keys, selected-day preference, autosave/drafts and failure handling remain.

Existing fullscreen stability, saved sidebar shortcuts, colouring imports, photo restoration/sharpness, weather cache scheduling and cast recovery code are retained. No new manifest entries, permissions or dependencies.

No Android build or device test was run. Verify CI, greeting/time-zone/day-boundary updates, QR scan/link, DLNA setup, schedule boundaries/wrapped times/manual overrides/restart, light-dark changes while editing and on media screens, calendar clipping/font scales/keyboard/selection/autosave allocations, and previous feature regressions.
